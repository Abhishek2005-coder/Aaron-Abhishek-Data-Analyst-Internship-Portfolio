What the tests showed

QuestionTestp-valueResult
Do men and women spend differently per order?Welch t-test0.45No difference
Does order value differ by category?ANOVA0.59No difference
Does category mix differ by gender?Chi-squared0.056Borderline, not significant
Does Electronics get more than 20% of orders?Binomialbelow 0.001Yes, 35.5%

Revenue is flat across 2025 with an August to September dip, and only 5.3% of 941 customer IDs ordered more than once. The recommendations: drop segment-specific pricing, protect Electronics, promote in August and September, and launch a loyalty programme.
